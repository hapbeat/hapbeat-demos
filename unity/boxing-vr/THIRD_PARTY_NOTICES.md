# Boxing asset provenance

1. Compact gloves and pictogram boxer: original procedural geometry authored in `Assets/Boxing/Editor/BoxingContent.cs`; ring and gym in `BoxingProject.cs`. No downloaded character, texture or animation assets. Unity built-in primitive meshes are construction primitives. Hapbeat-authored content uses the repository MIT license.
2. GloveSoft/Hard and BodySoft/Hard haptic pulses: original deterministic synthesis in `BoxingContent.HapticSamples`, 16 kHz PCM16 mono (MIT). Glove: short 155 Hz response, hard variant adds a second pulse. Body: longer 75 Hz response. These tactile clips are independent of the audible recordings.
3. Unity Engine, Universal Render Pipeline, XR Hands, OpenXR, XR Interaction Toolkit and Unity UI: development/runtime packages resolved by Unity Package Manager; their bundled licenses apply. They are not represented as CC0 assets. Unity's built-in `LegacyRuntime.ttf` is used for UI text.
4. Hapbeat SDK and Demo Switch: Hapbeat source code; respective repository/package licenses apply.
5. Editor-only XR Interaction Simulator: the core prefab, input actions and hand-expression data are imported from Unity XR Interaction Toolkit **3.3.1**, `Samples~/XR Interaction Simulator`. Unity's bundled package license applies (not CC0; copyright notice preserved in `Assets/Boxing/Editor/Simulator/LICENSE.md`). The sample UI and its art/scripts are not imported; UI/icon references were removed, and automatic removal of other HMD devices is disabled. Stale references to the classic hand-control asset were redirected to the included interaction hand-control asset with identical Action IDs. These utilities are isolated under `Assets/Boxing/Editor/Simulator`; player build reports verify they are not packaged in the game.

6. Audible impacts: Kenney, [Impact Sounds 1.0](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Downloaded 2026-09-14 from the official pack. `Audio/impactSoft_medium_000.ogg` and `impactSoft_heavy_000.ogg` are glove-contact sounds; `impactPunch_medium_000.ogg` and `impactPunch_heavy_000.ogg` are body-contact sounds. Original files are unchanged. Pack License.txt identifies Kenney and CC0; commercial use, modification and redistribution are allowed, attribution is not required.
7. Boxing bell: Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank, [Boxing bell #1, sound 1926](https://bigsoundbank.com/boxing-bell-1-s1926.html), released as CC0 on that page. Downloaded 2026-09-14 from `https://bigsoundbank.com/UPLOAD/bwf-en/1926.wav`. `Assets/Boxing/Audio/BoxingBellSource.wav` retains the recording; `BoxingGongFour.wav` is an edited four-strike sequence (0, 0.24, 0.48, 0.88 seconds) built from the final strike, with mono mixdown, peak normalization and tail fade. The same edited clip plays at round start and end. No endorsement implied.

The project's CC0 requirement applies to externally sourced game visual/audio content. The two audio sources above are CC0; code/original geometry remain MIT and Unity-licensed engine primitives/tooling retain their own licenses. No Ultimate Glove Ball/Meta game art or Unity XRI decorative sample art is included.
